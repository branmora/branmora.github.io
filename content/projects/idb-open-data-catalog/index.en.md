The IDB Open Data Catalog is where the Inter-American Development Bank shares data for anyone to reuse: datasets, research data linked to its publications, calculated indicators, and thematic data portals, with an API for the data and its metadata. The revamped catalog was launched on CKAN, the open-source data catalog software.

The move to CKAN took 14 months, in five phases: design, preparation, implementation, testing, and deployment. With Emelin Batres, I wrote about the new catalog on the IDB's Abierto al Público blog, and we presented the journey at CKAN Monthly Live on 2025-09-17.
